What the results mean

Assumptions: i.i.d. increments and independent simulated attackers. The Bernoulli standardized CLT requires 0 < p < 1. Finite samples fluctuate; convergence is asymptotic.

How to interpret the experiments

LLN: for every fixed tolerance ε > 0, the probability of |Sₙ/n − μ| > ε approaches zero as n grows. Variance: Var(Sₙ) = nσ², Var(Sₙ/n) = σ²/n and Var(Sₙ/√n) = σ². CLT: Rademacher Sₙ/√n converges in distribution to N(0,1); Bernoulli requires centering by np and scaling by √(np(1−p)). Attackers: increasing m improves Monte Carlo precision, not the per-attacker score distribution.

Finite-sample histograms can appear spiky because scores are discrete. The simulator models independent trials, not coordinated cyberattacks.
